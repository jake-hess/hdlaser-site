How we run the shop

Everything from unlocking the door to locking it, so any team member can run a full day the same way. The staff portal tracks the checklists, the work queue and your numbers; this page explains the why and the how.

Your first day

  1. Get your login. Hugh or a manager adds you to the team with your name and a PIN you choose. You sign in at hdlaser.net/staff. Your PIN is yours alone. Never use anyone else's, and never share yours.
  2. Get your Square passcode. You'll also be added to Square with your own 4-digit passcode for the register. Same rule: yours only. Square records every sale and refund under the person who did it.
  3. Read this page top to bottom, then shadow a full opening and a full closing with someone who has done it before.
  4. Safety first. Lasers and the UV printer are never left running unattended. The exhaust is on whenever a laser is cutting or engraving. Know where the fire extinguisher is before you touch a machine.
  5. Hours: Monday to Saturday 10 AM to 5 PM, Sunday 10 AM to 3 PM. The opener is in by 9:50. Clock in on the portal the moment you're inside.

Opening (9:50 to 10:15)

Tick each step in the portal's opening checklist as you go. The list there is the source of truth; this is what each step means.

  1. Unlock the front door, turn off the alarm.
  2. Clock in. This is how Hugh knows the shop is open. If nobody has clocked in by 10:15, the system texts Hugh and the on-call team member automatically.
  3. Back door open, lights on.
  4. Machines on: lasers, UV printer, exhaust, compressor. Let them warm up while you do the next steps. Run the UV printer's nozzle check if it sat idle over a day off.
  5. Check messages: contact@hdlaser.net and the shop phone. Every new web order and quote request also lands in the portal's work queue on its own.
  6. Review the work queue in the portal. It's already sorted by priority. Note what is due today.
  7. Register: count the drawer, open Square Point of Sale with your own passcode.
  8. Front of house: wipe counters, restock sample cups and the Grounds for Profit sheets at the front.

How the queue is ordered

Each job gets a score. Higher sits on top.

  • Due date is the biggest factor: overdue 100, due today 80, tomorrow 65, then lower the further out it is.
  • Order value adds up to 20 points, so a $1,000 order outranks a $40 one with the same due date.
  • Waiting time adds a point per day, so nothing gets forgotten.
  • Web orders still waiting for logo score low because there's nothing to do until the customer sends it. Nudge them by email every two days.

Walk-ins and same-day promises

The black banner at the top of the portal tells you how many more same-day jobs the shop can take right now. It counts the machine time left before closing (70% of the clock, the rest is customers and cleanup) minus everything already due today or tomorrow.

  1. Banner says 1 or more: you can promise same day. Log the job, take payment, start it in the queue.
  2. Banner says 0: promise pickup tomorrow or later. Never promise same day when the banner says 0, even for a friend. A late job costs more than a scheduled one.
  3. Big or unusual jobs (over 2 hours, new material, anything you're unsure about): quote a pickup date at least two days out and tell a manager.
  4. Always collect: customer name, phone number, the artwork or file, and what exactly goes where on the item. Take a photo of the item if it's theirs.

Checkout and refunds

  • Every sale goes through Square Point of Sale under your own passcode. Pick the item from the item list rather than "custom amount" wherever one exists, so the numbers stay useful.
  • Walk-in and phone jobs are paid in full at drop-off unless a manager says otherwise. Website cup orders are already paid.
  • Refunds for an overcharge or our mistake: do it in Square under your passcode, type the reason. Anything over $100, or any refund that isn't our mistake, needs a manager.
  • Custom orders are final once paid. That's in our Terms of Sale and the customer agreed to it. If a customer disputes it, be kind, don't argue, and hand it to a manager.
  • Resale customers (cafés buying cups to sell) give us their California seller's permit. If a web order shows Sales tax to invoice on the dashboard, a manager sends the tax invoice.

Production and pickup

  1. Tap Start on a job in the queue when you begin it. Tap Done when it's finished and packed. That's what feeds your numbers.
  2. Test on scrap before touching the customer's item whenever the material or settings are new to you.
  3. Check the finished piece against the order: spelling, placement, quantity. Then a second person checks it if one is in.
  4. Text or call the customer that it's ready. Bag it with their name on it and put it on the pickup shelf.
  5. For website cup orders, tap Done on the order in the queue. The customer gets pickup instructions.

Closing (last 30 minutes)

Tick each step in the portal's closing checklist.

  1. Finish or safely pause every running job. Mark done jobs as done.
  2. Reply to any customer waiting on a proof or a pickup time so nobody waits overnight.
  3. Close out Square: count the drawer. If it's over or short, write the amount in your clock-out note.
  4. Clean: laser beds, scrap out, UV printer wiped, floor swept.
  5. Machines off: lasers, UV printer, exhaust, compressor.
  6. Back door locked, lights off, alarm set.
  7. Clock out. Add a one-line note if anything unusual happened.
  8. Lock the front door.

If something goes wrong

  • You'll be late or can't come in: text Hugh before 9:30. If you're on call and you get the automatic "nobody has clocked in" text, reply 1 if you're on your way or 2 if you can't make it.
  • Machine fault, injury, or fire: people first, then machines. Call 911 for an injury or fire. Then call Hugh.
  • Upset customer: listen, don't argue, don't promise a refund. Get a manager, or take their number and have a manager call within the hour.
  • Portal or website down: keep working from Square and paper, and text Hugh. Log the jobs in the portal when it's back.

Managers and owner

  • See everyone's hours, shifts, on-time opens, checklist completion, jobs and job value in the portal's Team tab, and in the owner dashboard under Team.
  • Add team members, reset PINs, set who is on call, and deactivate people from the Team tab. Deactivating someone signs them out immediately.
  • Owner texts: Hugh gets a text when someone clocks in or out, when nobody has opened by 10:15, and for every new web order and payment. To change which staff events text him, edit the worker setting STAFF_ALERTS.
  • Weekly, Monday: review the Team table, the sales digest email, and the Needs attention lists on the dashboard.
